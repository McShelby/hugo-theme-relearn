Categories

The categories follow the Diátaxis documentation framework, which sorts each page by what the reader needs: learning, solving a task, understanding or looking something up.

This taxonmy page and their term pages are configured differently in comparison to the default. See the docs for what’s possible.

  • Explanation (25)
    Explanations give you the background on how things work and why they are the way they are.
  • Howto (59)
    How-to guides show you the steps to solve a specific task you already have in mind.
  • Reference (39)
    References describe things completely and precisely, for you to look up while you work.
  • Tutorial (3)
    Tutorials are lessons that take you step by step to a working result, so you learn by doing.
