Categorrries

Th' categories follow th' Diátaxis documentat'n framework, which sorts each plank by what th' reader needs: learn'n, solv'n a task, understand'n or look'n someth'n up.

This taxonmy plank an' their term planks be configured differently 'n comparison t' th' default. See th' docs fer what’s poss'ble.

    Explanat’ns give ye th’ background on how th’ns work an’ why they be th’ way they be.
